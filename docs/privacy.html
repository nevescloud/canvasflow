Kandue

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Kandue reads your Canvas LMS assignments and announcements, and turns them into ranked priorities, practice quizzes and reading aids. No backend of its own.

Data

  • Stays local. Courses, assignments, announcements, and settings live in chrome.storage.local and clear when you remove the extension. Canvas is read via /api/v1 on your existing browser session, and credentials are never seen, stored, or transmitted.
  • Your coursework is never transmitted. The only server Kandue contacts is your own school's Canvas, to read your assignments. Priorities are ranked inside the extension. Practice quizzes, summaries and translations all use Chrome's built-in AI model, which runs on your own computer, so the course material they read never leaves the device. There is no Kandue server, no account, and no third-party AI provider.

Not done

No tracking, analytics, cookies, or ads. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.
