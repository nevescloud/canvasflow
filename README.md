# Kandue

Every Canvas deadline in one panel. A Chrome extension for students on Canvas LMS: assignments from every course in one list, ranked by what needs you first, with practice quizzes and reading aids from Chrome's built-in AI on your own machine. No account to create and no server of its own, so your coursework stays in the browser.

[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/plodbfednbjngcmckbcilgpkhgijgado) · [Site](https://neves.cloud/kandue/) · [Privacy policy](https://neves.cloud/kandue/privacy.html)

## Feedback and requests

[Send feedback](https://neves.cloud/kandue/feedback.html) from the site, or [open an issue](https://github.com/nevescloud/kandue/issues/new/choose) here. The site's form fills the issue in for you and you press Create. Either way takes a free GitHub account, and issues are public, so leave out your name, email, and school login.
